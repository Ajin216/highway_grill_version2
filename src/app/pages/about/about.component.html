Located in the vibrant heart of Thenhipalam, Highway Grill is a cherished culinary haven where tradition meets innovation.

Known for our unwavering commitment to quality ingredients, delightful flavors, and exceptional service, Highway Grill invites you to savor a dining experience like no other. Our elegant interior is meticulously designed to provide a cozy yet refined ambiance, with warm lighting and tasteful decor that make every meal feel incredibly special.

Whether you’re joining us for a quick gourmet bite or a leisurely communal gathering, the warm, inviting atmosphere at Highway Grill ensures a memorable dining experience that satisfies all your senses.

Frequently Asked Questions

❖ Is the restaurant kids-friendly?

Yes! You can absolutely visit the restaurant with your kids in tow. Highway Grill is a fully kids-friendly venue offering specialized high chairs, comfortable family cabins, and mild spices customized for your little ones.

❖ What are your hours of operation?

Highway Grill is fully open and functional 7 days a week from **12:00 PM to 12:00 AM (Midnight)**. Whether it’s a late lunch, an early dinner, or a midnight craving, our kitchen is ready to welcome you with freshly prepared authentic dishes.
